# The Houdiscert Suite
The **Houdiscert Suite** is a web-app suite created by [houdert6](https://github.com/houdert6). It is comprised of three products, all under the domain `houdiscert.com`.

## houdiscert
[houdiscert](https://houdiscert.com) is the first and main product of The Houdiscert Suite. Its name is a portmanteau of "houdert6" and "Discord," which is why it is not under any subdomain. It was first suggested in February of 2025 and the domain was later registered in March of the same year. It uses the [React Framework](https://react.dev/) developed by [Meta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satan).
